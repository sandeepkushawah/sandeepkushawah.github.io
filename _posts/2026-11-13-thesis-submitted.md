I have submitted my PhD thesis, **"A Study of Structural and Dynamical Inhomogeneities in Supercooled Glass-Forming Model Liquids"**, at the Indian Institute of Technology Mandi, under the supervision of Dr. Prasanth P. Jose.

The thesis uses large-scale molecular dynamics simulations of the Kob–Andersen binary Lennard-Jones mixture to connect local structure with slow, heterogeneous dynamics:

- **Many-body structural entropy** — a four-point structural entropy S₃D = S₂ + S_Ω showing that most of the structural order missed by g(r) is orientational ([Soft Matter, 2026](/publications/3-beyond-pair-entropy/)).
- **Dynamic heterogeneity at the mode-coupling crossover** — mobile and immobile particles, percolating clusters and string-like motion compared across four densities ([project](/portfolio/portfolio-2/)).
- **Molecular cages and density relaxation** — how the first peak of g(r) carries the mean relaxation time, and how attraction acts on it ([project](/portfolio/portfolio-3/)).

The thesis is now with the examiners. Figures and animations from the work are in the [research gallery](/gallery/).
