Our paper **"Breakdown of Stokes–Einstein–Debye relations at the gas–supercooled liquid coexistence"** has been accepted for publication in *Soft Matter* (Royal Society of Chemistry).

**Authors:** Devansu Chakraborty, Sandeep Kushawah, Manoj Rana and Prasanth P. Jose

The Stokes–Einstein–Debye relations connect how fast molecules diffuse and rotate to the structural relaxation of the liquid around them, and in supercooled liquids they break down. Using molecular dynamics of FENE-bonded dumbbell molecules, with a dense liquid slab coexisting with a dilute vapour formed by cavitation, we separate the two mechanisms behind that breakdown — translational–rotational decoupling and the failure of the Debye rotational-diffusion model — and find that they have opposite density dependences: Debye breakdown is stronger at high density, decoupling at low density.

**Update, November 2026:** the paper is now published online — [doi:10.1039/D6SM00374E](https://doi.org/10.1039/D6SM00374E).

This is my second paper in *Soft Matter* this year, after [Beyond Pair Entropy](/publications/3-beyond-pair-entropy/).
