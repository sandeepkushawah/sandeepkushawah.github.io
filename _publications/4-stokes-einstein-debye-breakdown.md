**Abstract (summary).** The breakdown of Stokes–Einstein–Debye (SED) relations in supercooled glass-forming liquids is primarily driven by two distinct mechanisms: translational–rotational decoupling and the failure of the Debye rotational diffusion model. This work examines both at the coexistence of a supercooled liquid with its gas phase.

Published in *Soft Matter* (Royal Society of Chemistry), November 2026 — [doi:10.1039/D6SM00374E](https://doi.org/10.1039/D6SM00374E).
